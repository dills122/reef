# Calcify capacity profile: what the finite bounds are and what replaces them

Date: 2026-10-14. Status: **working spec; decisions below were taken by the implementation task under the owner's delegation of 2026-10-14 (design choices that are not a major refactor or pivot), recorded here so they can be overturned.** Nothing here is accepted authority and none of it changes the finite P3 path.

## Why this exists
The finite P3 profile is one closed run: one instrument, one session, at most 16 parties, at most 128 captures. The arena goalpost (10,000 booked trades/s, 5 to 60 minute sessions, thousands of participants, 64 instruments) cannot be expressed in it. The commitment design ([state commitment design](CALCIFY_FINANCE_STATE_COMMITMENT_DESIGN.md)) says how the ledger scales; this note lists **every pinned bound** that a capacity profile has to replace, where it lives, and which stage owns it, so the work can be cut into small reviewable pieces.

## Decisions taken (defaults; overturn by editing this table)
1. **A capacity profile is a new profile id in a new binding schema (v3)**; binding v1 and v2, their goldens and the finite code paths stay byte-identical. The projection schema is shared (one set of tables; the bound is per binding).
2. **The rotation unit is a slice of about 5 minutes** (6 million decisions, about 15 million entities, about 5.5 GiB in one ledger at the contract rate), sealed by `(final sequence, C(S), chain head)` that the next slice's genesis references. Default `sequence_bound` for a slice: 8,000,000. A 60-minute session is twelve slices.
3. **Accounts are state, not configuration.**
4. **The projection bound is enforced in the database, not only by the application**, so a defect in the projector cannot overrun a binding (this PR).
5. **Foreign keys on the projection stay** (measured: not measurably slower with set-based writes, CAL-FIN-P13).

## What this PR (migration 0016) changes
`boundary.calcify_finance_checkpoint.sequence_bound` (default 320, 320..10^9, immutable); the checkpoint CHECK becomes `last_sequence BETWEEN 0 AND sequence_bound`; the decision row CHECK `BETWEEN 1 AND 320` becomes `>= 1` plus a statement-level trigger that refuses any insert beyond the binding's bound (no measurable cost: 300,000 rows in 9.3 s with it, 10.3 s without, same machine, run order dominates the difference). `FinanceProjector.open/claim` take the bound (default 320) and refuse an existing binding opened with another. Finite behaviour is unchanged: all 142 finance tests pass, including the broker and end-to-end suites, and a new test shows a finite binding still refuses row 321 and a capacity binding refuses its own bound plus one.

## Decisions taken in this stage (defaults; overturn by editing)
6. **Lanes are instrument groups (option S1 of the sharding note), cash is per lane.** A participant's cash is allocated to lanes by funding controls; a rebalance control between lanes is not built yet.
7. **Instant settlement profile:** the decoder follows every captured execution with its first settlement attempt (`<executionId>/1`, operator authority). A failed attempt leaves the obligation pending and is repaired by an operator control with a later attempt number. Two decision modes, chosen per plan by `decisionMode` (part of the lane binding; a live lane is never switched in place):
   - `two-step` (the default; every existing binding, digest and golden is unchanged): a booked trade is two inputs and two decisions, the `Capture` and a `Settle` of attempt 1.
   - `capture-settle-v1` (opt-in, owner-approved 2026-10-15, OFF by default): a booked trade is **one input, `CaptureSettle`, and one decision** (see [the combined-decision investigation](CALCIFY_COMBINED_DECISION_INVESTIGATION.md) for the measurements that motivated it). One action key `("calcify-finance","capture-settle",<executionId>)`, one `K` leaf, one sequence number, one state root, one chain link per trade, so **the sequence counts trades**. The recorded disposition is the settle outcome (`SETTLED`, `INSUFFICIENT_CASH`, `INSUFFICIENT_SHARES`, `BALANCE_OVERFLOW`); every capture failure (unknown account, invalid input, overflow, redelivery, conflict, exhausted capture or obligation budget) refuses the whole trade and records nothing, so there is no half-booked trade and no committed post-capture state. A shortfall leaves the obligation `PENDING` and writes the attempt leaf `A(execution,1)`, so an operator `Settle(execution, 2)` works live and after restore exactly as after the two-step flow. A trade that settles at once writes **no attempt leaf** (it would never be consulted: a later `Settle` is refused `ALREADY_SETTLED` before the attempt check); the projection still gets its attempt row, derived from the decision, so the read tables are identical to the two-step flow's. The attempt budget (`maxSettleAttempts`) therefore counts attempt leaves only and is checked only for a trade that would write one. Redelivery is benign (`PRIOR_RESULT`, or `EXECUTION_ALREADY_CAPTURED` for the same trade under another key, including a trade booked the two-step way). Decision checksum layout, `CapacityChain.verifyLink`, entity classes and encodings, and the state commitment are unchanged. Not comparable with a two-step chain of the same trades (different entity set and sequence).
8. **No per-record history list in the capacity profile.** The store verifies itself (entities hash to the head decision's committed state) and the launcher requires each restored lane to be exactly the head of its published decision log. Redelivery is absorbed by the ledger's action keys.
9. **Capture envelope for the capacity profile is the existing `FiniteLifecycleCaptureV1`** (schema, binding digest and content digest checked; the digest check can be switched off). A capacity-specific capture contract and binding v3 are still to do.
